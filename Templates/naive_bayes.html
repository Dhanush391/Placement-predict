Bayesian Generative Formulation

Posterior Calculation

P(Placement = 1 | X) ∝ P(Placement = 1) · ∏ P(xᵢ | Placement = 1)
Gaussian distribution assumption: P(xᵢ | y) = (1 / √(2πσ²)) · exp(- (xᵢ - μ)² / (2σ²))

Advantages & Limitations

Extremely fast O(N·d) training with zero iterative optimization required. Robust against irrelevant features, though correlated features (e.g. CGPA & SGPA) violate independence assumptions.
