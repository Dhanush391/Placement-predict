Data Transformation & Leakage Prevention Layer

Before feeding raw student data into supervised models, this pipeline transforms features into optimal mathematical representations. It guarantees zero training-serving skew by encapsulating imputation, scaling, and categorical encoding inside a reusable ColumnTransformer.

Missing Value Imputation: Median (Numeric) & Mode (Categorical)
M2 Scaling: StandardScaler & MinMaxScaler
Categorical Encoding: One-Hot & Ordinal Encoders

1. Missing Value Imputation

Real-world student records have absent exam scores or unrecorded workshop counts. Imputation strategies prevent discarding valid student observations.

Strategy Applied:
  • Numerical features: SimpleImputer(strategy='median') — robust to extreme outlier scores.
  • Categorical features: SimpleImputer(strategy='most_frequent') — preserves high-frequency modes.
Leakage Guard: Imputers fit strictly on the training partition and only transform the test/inference partitions.

2. Feature Scaling: M2 vs M3

Compares StandardScaler ($\mu=0, \sigma=1$) and MinMaxScaler ($[0, 1]$).

M2 Linear Models / GD: Scale Sensitive!

Gradient descent oscillates wildly if Attendance (range 0-100) dominates CGPA (range 0-10) by $10\times$.

M3 Tree-Based Models: Scale Invariant

Decision tree greedy splits are monotonic; $x > 7.5$ yields the identical entropy decrease whether $x$ is scaled or raw.

3. Categorical Encodings

Prevents the "arithmetic trap" of label encoding on nominal attributes.

Encoders Deployed:
  • One-Hot (Nominal): City, Stream, Specialisation. Avoids imposing fake distances (e.g. Pune - Delhi = 6).
  • Ordinal (Ranked): CollegeTier (Tier3 < Tier2 < Tier1), CGPA_Tier (Low < Mid < High). Preserves true monotonic hierarchies.
handle_unknown="ignore" prevents inference crashes on unseen categories.
