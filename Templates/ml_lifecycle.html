The Training-Serving Boundary & Production Serving Architecture

Production Serving

Why Training-Serving Skew is the #1 Production Bug

Training-serving skew occurs when the code or data distributions used during model training diverge from live inference. Examples include:

  • Feature Leakage: Using semester 8 grades during training to predict an internship drive occurring in semester 6.
  • Inconsistent Scaling: Calculating mean and std globally across all 50k records rather than fitting only on the training split.
  • Unseen Encodings: A new city or university tier appearing in live traffic with no mapped one-hot dummy column.

Integrated REST Serving Endpoints

Live predictions and audits are served through integrated low-latency JSON REST endpoints:

POST /api/predict -> Live student placement inference
POST /api/v1/trace-prediction -> End-to-end latency profiling
POST /api/v1/skew-audit -> Schema & bounds validation
GET /api/v1/online-features/<id> -> Feature store entity lookup
